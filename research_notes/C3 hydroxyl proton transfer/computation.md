# Computational evidence for the C3 hydroxyl proton acceptor

## What did the principal MDD QM/MM study actually test?

### Takeaway
McClory and colleagues found a feasible ATP γ-phosphate-assisted phosphorylation path in one reconstructed Staphylococcus epidermidis MDD–MVAPP–ATP–two-Mg model. They did **not** perform a matched activation-barrier comparison against an optimized Asp-assisted path: they explicitly declined further study of the base mechanism because Asp283 was distant from the hydroxyl in their selected MD conformation.

### Cited Findings
- The primary paper is McClory J, Hui C, Zhang J, Huang M. *The phosphorylation mechanism of mevalonate diphosphate decarboxylase: a QM/MM study.* Organic & Biomolecular Chemistry 2020;18:518–529. DOI 10.1039/C9OB02254F; first published December 11, 2019. The system is **MDD with MVAPP**, not PMD with MVAP. [Publisher](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f), [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31854421/).
- I read the complete twelve-page publisher PDF already present at `/nfs/lambda_stor_01/homes/rzhu/PMD/QMMM modeling/paper/McClory et al. - 2020 - The phosphorylation mechanism of mevalonate diphosphate decarboxylase a QMMM study.pdf`. Relevant printed pages: methods 519; model development 521–523; omission of Asp pathway and QM-region rationale 524–525; mechanistic drawing and transition-state validation 526; barrier 527. The open author manuscript is another version of the **same study**, not independent corroboration. [Author manuscript](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/files/195799974/MDD_2Mg_Manusctript_Final_revised_R2_1_.pdf).
- Starting coordinates were reconstructed from mutant MDD/ATPγS structure 4DPW with the WT substrate pose from 4DPT; ATPγS was changed to ATP and residue283 restored to Asp. The methods paragraph internally calls the starting variant “D283W” but then describes restoring an alanine to Asp; the relevant structural study identifies D283A. This is an internal naming inconsistency, not evidence that a Trp model was studied. The authors manually placed Mg and water ligands by analogy to pyruvate kinase 3HQP because MDD metal positions had not been established when their model was built. [McClory methods, printed p519](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f), [source structural paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4227304/).
- Four 100ns MD replicas used AMBER14/ff14SB, GAFF for ligands, RESP charges from HF/6-31G(d), TIP3P water, 300K and a 1bar production ensemble. PROPKA/manual inspection assigned protonation; reported predicted pKa values were Asp283 0.4 and Lys188 14.7, corresponding to anionic Asp and cationic Lys at pH7. They tried multiple one- and two-Mg arrangements; some one-Mg structures separated substrates or developed abnormal coordination, and one did not yield a phosphoryl-transfer transition state in subsequent scans. [McClory pp519–523](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).
- QM/MM used ONIOM with **electronic embedding**, B3LYP/6-31+G(d) for the QM layer and Amber Parm99 for MM, a cluster extending12Å from the site and atoms outside6Å frozen. QM included MVAPP, methyl-triphosphate in place of full ATP, both Mg ions, coordinating waters, and sidechains of Ser107, Ser141, Lys188, Ser192 and Asp283; net QM charge−4. [McClory methods p519 and region discussion p525](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).
- The reaction coordinate was shortened in0.1Å increments: substrate C3-hydroxyl oxygen to ATP Pγ. A structure from this potential-energy scan was optimized as a TS and had one imaginary frequency, **−191.99cm⁻¹**. The authors report both incoming O–Pγ and departing Pγ–Oβ3 distances as **2.04Å** at the TS. Their graphical reaction scheme explicitly transfers the hydroxyl proton to a **nonbridging terminal γ-phosphate oxygen**, labeled **Oγ2** in Figure6, rather than to Oβ3 or to Asp. [McClory pp525–526, Figures5–6](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).
- The authors report an **estimated Gibbs activation barrier of17.4kcal/mol**, compared with an experimental rate-derived estimate of15.5kcal/mol. They classify the modeled path as concerted SN2 with a dissociative TS character; estimated fractional bond number0.31, described as69% dissociative. This is the paper's stationary-structure calculation, **not an independently sampled QM/MM free-energy PMF across enzyme conformations**. The main text describes PES scans and frequency validation; the detailed thermochemical decomposition was not independently reconstructed here. [McClory p527, Figure7](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).
- Most important limitation: after noting the distance between Asp283 and the C3-OH in their MD model, the authors state that “further study of catalytic base mechanism was not carried out” (12 words). There is therefore **no verified Asp-assisted barrier in this paper** to compare with17.4kcal/mol. [McClory p525](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).

### Inferences
- This establishes **model-dependent plausibility** for the phosphate-assisted path and provides useful mechanistic hypotheses about Mg/water/Lys/Asp organization. It does not establish a free-energy preference over all Asp-assisted, water-mediated or mixed routes.
- Agreement of an estimated barrier with a turnover-derived number cannot uniquely identify a proton acceptor; distinct mechanisms can yield similar apparent barriers and overall turnover may mix chemical and conformational effects.
- The distant Asp geometry used to exclude the alternative is itself a result of their reconstructed metal arrangement and conformational sampling. A separate Asp-near active-site ensemble is not ruled out by that result.
- Terminal γ-oxygen names can be permuted by ligand builders. The closest RFD4 nonbridging γ oxygen is a transparent geometric proxy, but should not automatically be equated with the atom called Oγ2 in this paper without a spatial/coordination mapping.

### Gaps
- Supplementary PDF and PDB are listed on the publisher page but their actual file links could not be retrieved through the available web endpoint. I have not independently reproduced their stationary-point thermochemistry or inspected all provided optimized coordinates.
- No optimized Asp-assisted path, matched ΔΔG‡, ensemble uncertainty or direct isotope-based proton-acceptor validation is reported in the principal computational paper.
- The main text describes a basis-set correction as6-31++G(d,p) in methods and6-311++G(d,p) in results; do not silently select one as verified without consulting its SI. This does not change the central limitation above.

## Is there independent computational confirmation or contradiction?

### Takeaway
The focused search found one detailed peer-reviewed MDD QM/MM mechanism paper that favors the γ-phosphate route. Other retrieved material either cites that work, concerns different kinase reactions, or reports subsequent work in progress rather than an independent MDD proton-acceptor comparison. This is not evidence of a settled computational consensus.

### Cited Findings
- Chen and colleagues' 2020 experimental structural study explicitly connects its dissociative phosphoryl-transfer interpretation with the recent McClory calculation. It supplies independent crystallographic and biochemical observations, but it is **not another QM/MM comparison of Asp versus ATP proton acceptance**. It also discusses a strong difference between the degree of dissociative character inferred from its transition-state-analogue structure and McClory's computed structure; those estimates come from different chemical states and should not be treated as identical results. [Chen et al., Nature Communications2020, DOI10.1038/s41467-020-17733-0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7414129/).
- A 2021 conference abstract, “Reaction mechanism of Mevalonate diphosphate decarboxylases using QM/MM,” by Sónia F.G.Santos, Gary Black, Warispreet Singh and Meilan Huang, says that magnesium coordination and water locations remain uncertain, describes MD conformations as starting points for phosphorylation/decarboxylation QM/MM work, and cites McClory and Chen. It does **not** report an acceptor assignment or a relative barrier. It shares Huang as an author with McClory, so even a completed extension would not be an independent laboratory replication. [Conference programme, poster2, p16](https://www.mgms.org/WordPress/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/YMF2020-21_programme_26012021.pdf).
- A related 2019 QM/MM paper from McClory and collaborators revisits **mevalonate kinase**, which phosphorylates the **C5** hydroxyl of mevalonate. It is informative about GHMP alternatives but is not another study of PMD/MDD C3 chemistry and is not independent of the same computational group. [DOI10.1039/C8OB03197E](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2019/ob/c8ob03197e).
- The local project plan explicitly **assumed** substrate-assisted transfer following McClory. Local structural-audit notes document alternative Lys protonation, Mg-shell exchange across a model boundary, solvent/basin changes and constrained-scan issues, and require saddle/mode/IRC checks before accepting barriers. These are local diagnostic calculations, not independent validated evidence settling the mechanism. [Local plan](/nfs/lambda_stor_01/homes/rzhu/PMD/QMMM%20modeling/qmmm/docs/PLAN.md), [local structural review](/nfs/lambda_stor_01/homes/rzhu/PMD/QMMM%20modeling/qmmm/analysis/structural_audit/REVIEW.txt).

### Inferences
- The fair evidence statement is “a published QM/MM model supports ATP-assisted transfer, consistent with some non-computational observations,” not “several independent computations establish the route.”
- A review's schematic, the paper's PubMed entry, its author manuscript and its publisher PDF are all presentations of the same calculation and must not be counted separately.

### Gaps
- Searches on2026-11-13 for the exact enzyme name combined with proton transfer, QM/MM, DFT, free energy, Asp283 and later-year terms did not identify a second peer-reviewed MDD or PMD head-to-head QM/MM study. This is a bounded search outcome, not a claim that no such work exists.
- No published computational study specific to the user's de novo RFD4 PMD structures was found or expected; no mechanism-specific PMF/barrier has been calculated for these structures here.

## What does this imply for the RFD4 analysis and the next discriminating calculation?

### Takeaway
It is justified to retain both Asp-assisted and γ-phosphate-assisted routes in the structural audit. Heavy-atom distances can filter grossly inaccessible arrangements, but cannot rank activation free energies, establish the proton orientation, or convert generated geometry into evidence of a better catalyst.

### Cited Findings
- McClory's favored route couples proton transfer to phosphorylation, while Asp participates indirectly through a metal-coordinated water network; its definition is more specific than an isolated “first deprotonation” event. [McClory Figures5–6 and text pp525–526](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ob/c9ob02254f).
- The independent 2020 MDD structural paper distinguishes Asp's possible orientation/decarboxylation roles and notes that the proton acceptor remains uncertain even while suggesting a transferred phosphate oxygen could participate. [Chen et al. mechanistic discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7414129/).
- The local project audit specifically requires matched reactant protonation, complete first-shell Mg ligands inside the reactive region, model-region checks, and validated saddle/IRC endpoints rather than interpreting scan peaks as reaction barriers. [Local review](/nfs/lambda_stor_01/homes/rzhu/PMD/QMMM%20modeling/qmmm/analysis/structural_audit/REVIEW.txt).

### Inferences
- For structural reporting, keep distinct observables: (i) O_nucleophile–Pγ and O_nucleophile–Pγ–Oβγ for phosphoryl-transfer alignment, (ii) O_nucleophile–each nonbridging γ-oxygen and their Mg contacts for substrate-assisted possibilities, (iii) O_nucleophile–both Asp carboxylate oxygens, plus plausible intervening waters, for direct or relayed protein-base possibilities. A nearest-oxygen minimum is not a mechanism probability.
- Explicit hydrogens with defensible protonation/tautomer assignments are needed to assess O–H···O directions and donor–H/acceptor–H distances. Short O···O contacts alone can reflect poor rotamers, electrostatics or a nonproductive state.
- A useful mechanism test would compare both routes **in the same enzyme–substrate system**, with equivalent reactant protonation, Mg stoichiometry/coordination, solvent treatment, QM region and embedding, multiple starting conformations, and converged sampling. A two-dimensional free-energy surface should include a phosphoryl-transfer coordinate and a proton-transfer difference coordinate. Where a water relay is feasible, include it rather than forcing a binary direct-Asp/direct-phosphate choice.
- Each proposed path needs a stable reactant basin and verified chemical endpoints; static TS calculations need the relevant imaginary mode and IRC, while free-energy calculations need convergence/replication and uncertainty. Solvent or metal-shell reorganization must not masquerade as the chemical barrier.
- Until those tests, one can use ATP-assisted transfer as a plausible literature-motivated hypothesis while retaining Asp assistance; assigning a numerical likelihood or declaring one RFD4 design mechanistically superior would overstate the evidence.

### Gaps
- The current heavy-atom RFD4 coordinates do not establish proton locations, pKa shifts, reaction barriers or solvent-mediated pathways. General MDD/MVAPP evidence does not by itself establish PMD/MVAP or de novo scaffold mechanisms.
